How I work

  • 01Discover. Learn how the business actually works: interview the people doing it, read the real paperwork, watch the workflow itself.
  • 02Decompose. Find the boundaries that make the problem manageable. Decide what to preserve, what to isolate, and where complexity should live.
  • 03Build & integrate. Implement what is missing, integrate what should be retained. I write the production code myself.
  • 04Production. Stay involved through migration, rollout, training, and adoption. A solution is finished when it is operating.
